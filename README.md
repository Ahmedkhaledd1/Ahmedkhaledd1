## 🚀 About Me

Computer Engineering graduate from Ain Shams University & University of East London, specialized in **Generative AI** and **Machine Learning**. I'm passionate about building systems that bridge the gap between human language and data  from RAG pipelines that chat with databases, to transformer models that summarize and generate text.

- 🔭 I'm currently working on **RAG-based systems** for enterprise applications
- 🌱 I'm diving deeper into **LLM fine-tuning** and **advanced prompt engineering**
- 👯 I'm looking to collaborate on **open-source AI projects** and **NLP research**
- 💬 Ask me about **Generative AI, RAG architectures, or building with LangChain**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I built a smart home system that predicts what you want to watch based on your mood!

## 💻 What I Do

- 🤖 Design **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** pipelines for accurate, grounded AI responses
- 🗣️ Build **natural language interfaces** that convert user queries into actionable database insights
- 📊 Develop **predictive models** for time-series analysis and activity recognition
- 🔧 Fine-tune **transformer models** (BART, LLaMA, GPT variants) for specialized tasks
- ☁️ Deploy AI solutions with **Docker** and cloud-ready architectures
